Did Snapchat Turn Your Friends Into a Solar System? Here's How Snapchat Planets Really Work

If you have Snapchat+, you may have noticed something unusual when viewing a friend's profile. Instead of seeing a simple Best Friends ranking, Snapchat turns your closest friends into planets.

You become the Sun.

Your closest friend becomes Mercury.

Your eighth closest friend becomes Neptune.

This feature is called [Snapchat Planets](https://snaporbitlab.com), officially known as the Friend Solar System. It's one of the most popular [Snapchat+ features](https://snaporbitlab.com/snapchat-plus-features/) because it gives users a visual way to see who they interact with the most.

But what do Snapchat Planets actually mean? How does Snapchat decide who gets Mercury and who ends up as Neptune?

Here's everything you need to know.

What Are Snapchat Planets?

Snapchat Planets are part of the Friend Solar System, a feature available exclusively to [Snapchat+](https://snaporbitlab.com/snapchat-plus-guide/) subscribers.
The system is based on your [Best Friends list](https://snaporbitlab.com/snapchat-best-friends-list/). Snapchat looks at your interactions and places your top eight friends in a solar system.

In this system:

You are the Sun.
Your closest friend is Mercury.
Your second closest friend is Venus.
Your third closest friend is Earth.
The ranking continues until Neptune at position eight.
The more you interact with someone on Snapchat, the closer their planet appears to your Sun.
These rankings are not permanent. They can change as your activity changes.

For example, if you chat, send Snaps, reply to Stories, and maintain streaks with one friend more often than others, that person may move closer to the top of your Friend Solar System.

Snapchat Planets Order and Meaning

Each planet represents a different position in your Snapchat Best Friends ranking.

Mercury (#1 Best Friend)
Mercury is your closest Snapchat friend.
You exchange the most Snaps, chats, and interactions with this person. They are currently your number one Best Friend on Snapchat.

Venus (#2 Best Friend)
Venus represents your second closest friend.
You interact with them frequently, but not quite as much as your Mercury friend.

Earth (#3 Best Friend)
Earth is your third closest Snapchat friend.
You stay in regular contact, but your interactions are slightly lower than your top two friends.

Mars (#4 Best Friend)
Mars represents a friend you interact with often.
You may exchange Snaps regularly, but the overall engagement is lower than your top three connections.

Jupiter (#5 Best Friend)
Jupiter is the middle of your Best Friends ranking.
You still communicate with this person, but less frequently than the friends above them.

Saturn (#6 Best Friend)
Saturn represents a friend you interact with occasionally.
They remain in your top eight, but activity is relatively limited.

Uranus (#7 Best Friend)
Uranus is the seventh position in the Friend Solar System.
You still engage with this friend, but not enough to move them higher in the rankings.

Neptune (#8 Best Friend)
Neptune is the final planet in the Snapchat solar system.
This friend has the lowest level of interaction among your current top eight friends.

How Does Snapchat Decide Planet Rankings?

Snapchat has never publicly revealed the exact formula behind the Friend Solar System.
However, the feature appears to be connected to the same activity signals used for the Best Friends list.

Several factors likely influence Snapchat Planet rankings:

Snaps sent and received
Chat conversations
Story views
Story replies
Snap Streak activity
How often both users respond to each other
Recent interaction patterns
Overall consistency of communication
One thing many users misunderstand is that streaks alone do not determine your planet position.

For example, someone can have a long Snap Streak but still appear as Saturn, Uranus, or Neptune if most interactions are limited to maintaining that streak.

The Friend Solar System seems to reward overall engagement rather than a single activity type.

Why Did My Snapchat Planet Change?

Many users notice their planet position changing and assume something is wrong.
In most cases, this is completely normal.
Snapchat rankings update based on recent activity. If you start interacting more with another friend, their planet may move closer to the Sun.
Likewise, if communication slows down, a friend may move lower in the rankings.
This is why someone who was Mercury last week could become Earth or Mars later.
The Friend Solar System is designed to reflect current interaction patterns, not permanent friendships.

Why Did Snapchat Planets Disappear?

If Snapchat Planets suddenly disappeared, several things may have happened:

Your Snapchat+ subscription expired.
The Friend Solar System feature was turned off in settings.
You are no longer in that person's top eight friends.
The app needs an update.
Snapchat is testing changes to the feature.

In most situations, disappearing planets do not mean you were blocked or removed as a friend.

Who Can See Your Snapchat Planets?

A common question is whether other people can see your Friend Solar System.
The answer is no.
Your Snapchat Planets are private.
Nobody can open your profile and view your complete solar system.

A friend may only see their own position if the Friend Solar System is available to them. They cannot see where your other friends rank.
It's also important to understand that rankings are not always the same in both directions.
You might be someone's Mercury while they appear as your Earth or Mars.
Snapchat calculates friend rankings separately for every account.
Snapchat Planets vs Snap Score vs Streaks
These features are often confused, but they measure different things.
Snapchat Planets show who you interact with most.
Snap Score shows overall Snapchat activity and usage.
Snap Streaks show how many consecutive days two users have exchanged Snaps.
Having a high Snap Score does not automatically make someone your Mercury.
Likewise, a long streak alone does not guarantee a top ranking.
The Friend Solar System considers multiple types of interaction.

Common Snapchat Planet Myths

Myth: Mercury Means You're Best Friends Forever
Not necessarily.
Planet rankings change as interaction levels change.
Myth: My Planet Disappeared Because I Was Blocked
Usually not.
Subscription changes and ranking updates are much more common reasons.
Myth: Snapchat Planets Update Instantly
No.
Rankings update periodically and may not change immediately after a single Snap.
Myth: Friends Can See Their Exact Rank In My Solar System
They can only see their own position, not your entire Friend Solar System.
Myth: Streaks Alone Control Planet Rankings
Streaks help, but Snapchat appears to consider chats, Story engagement, Snaps, and overall activity as well.

What Do Snapchat Planets Really Tell You?

Snapchat Planets are meant to show activity, not friendship quality.
A close real-life friend might appear as Neptune simply because they rarely use Snapchat.
On the other hand, someone you message every day could appear as Mercury because most of your communication happens inside the app.
The Friend Solar System is best viewed as a fun Snapchat+ feature rather than a measure of how much someone values your friendship.
Think of it as a snapshot of your recent Snapchat activity, not a report card for your relationships.
